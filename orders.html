How it works

Bid → executable quote → payment → shipment

Think of this as a simple, procurement-friendly auction process. You tell us what you need and what you’re willing to pay. We confirm specs, availability, freight, and payment method.

1) Submit your bid

Share who you are, ship-to ZIP, quantity, and your target price.

  • Sealed bid (not public)
  • Fast follow-up for clarifications

2) Receive an executable quote

If accepted, we send a quote you can execute immediately.

  • Invoice + payment link
  • Specs + delivery window

3) Pay & fulfill

Pay by credit card or alternative method and we schedule shipping.

  • Credit cards accepted
  • ACH/wire and other methods available
