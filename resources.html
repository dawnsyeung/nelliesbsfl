Research Pillars

Evidence Base for Integrated BSFL Solutions

These pillars summarize what the literature is converging on and what’s still actively being tested. Each section links back to citations so you can follow the methods—not just the headline.

Organic Waste Valorization

Studies evaluate substrate conversion efficiency, pathogen suppression, and emissions profiles when food waste and manures are processed by larvae.

  • Quantify diversion rates vs. composting and anaerobic digestion
  • Benchmark greenhouse gas savings and biosecure handling
  • Refine feeding regimes for high-moisture waste streams

Animal Nutrition & Health

Larval meal and oil integrations are validated across poultry, swine, aquaculture, and companion animal diets to improve feed conversion, immunity, and product quality.

  • Measure digestibility of proteins, lipids, and functional compounds
  • Track microbiome shifts and gut barrier integrity
  • Compare performance against fishmeal, soybean, and rendered fats

Frass for Soil & Ecology

Field trials and soil biology assessments document how insect frass reintroduces nutrients, microbes, and chitin-derived elicitors that improve plant vigor and resilience.

  • Monitor crop yield and quality responses
  • Evaluate microbial biodiversity and enzymatic activity
  • Assess soil structure, carbon retention, and water use efficiency

Systems & Lifecycle Assessment

Cross-disciplinary work models environmental and economic performance of distributed BSFL infrastructure, informing permitting and ESG reporting for municipal and industrial partners.

  • Compare lifecycle impacts across waste management pathways
  • Map circular value flows for co-products and residues
  • Support investment cases with sensitivity analysis

Research Library (Curated)

The BSFL Evidence Base — What Buyers, Cities, and Regulators Trust

Below are peer-reviewed papers and widely-cited technical resources focused on outcomes that matter in practice: feed performance, functional health effects, waste diversion, lifecycle impacts, and bankable economics. Every item is chosen for “benefits” clarity—not hype.

Core nutritional value & feed performance

  • Nutritional value of black soldier fly (Hermetia illucens) larvae (BSFL) processed by three different methods of treatment (2022, open access) — Shows how processing choices shift nutrient quality, helping you specify the right product form (meal, oil, dried larvae) for each feed buyer.
  • The nutritive value of black soldier fly larvae reared on common organic waste streams (2019, Scientific Reports) — Connects substrate choice to nutrient outcomes, supporting consistent specs even when your inputs vary.
  • Black Soldier Fly Larvae as a Novel Protein Feed Resource for Sustainable Animal Production: A Systematic Review (2025, open access) — A high-signal synthesis across species that makes the investment case: where BSFL performs, what constraints matter, and what “good” looks like at scale.
  • The potential of black soldier fly (Hermetia illucens L.) larvae in chicken and swine nutrition: a review (2024) — A practical guide for two of the biggest markets, summarizing inclusion considerations and performance outcomes that procurement teams care about.
  • Improved nutritional and antioxidant properties of black soldier fly larvae through processing (2024, ScienceDirect) — Supports premium positioning: processing can meaningfully improve stability and functional properties relevant to feed quality and shelf life.
  • Sustainable use of Hermetia illucens insect biomass for feed and food (ScienceDirect) — Bridges product value and sustainability: a useful anchor when you need to communicate both performance and lifecycle benefits in one narrative.

Animal health, immunity & functional compounds

  • Immunomodulatory potential of black soldier fly larvae (2022) — Highlights why BSFL is more than “protein”: bioactive fractions (e.g., lipids and peptides) that can support immune resilience and gut function.
  • Review: A journey into the black soldier fly digestive system (2025, ScienceDirect) — Explains the biology behind conversion efficiency and functional outputs, useful when technical stakeholders ask “why does this work so well?”
  • Sections on antimicrobial peptides, lauric acid, and immune enhancement within Black Soldier Fly Larvae as a Novel Protein Feed Resource for Sustainable Animal Production: A Systematic Review (2025, open access) — A ready-made “functional benefits” reference for decks: antimicrobial peptides + lauric acid are frequently cited mechanisms behind animal-health interest.
  • The immunological and antimicrobial section of Black Soldier Fly: A Keystone Species for the Future of Sustainable Bioeconomy (2025, open access) — A strong umbrella citation that connects functional compounds to real-world bioeconomy applications (feed, waste, and co-products).

Waste conversion, circularity & organic waste management

  • Material flow analysis and life cycle assessment of food waste bioconversion by black soldier fly larvae (Hermetia illucens L.) (2020, ScienceDirect) — A decision-grade view of where the mass goes (larvae, residue, emissions), useful for designing systems that maximize value capture.
  • Bioconversion of biowaste by black soldier fly larvae (Hermetia illucens): Life cycle assessment of environmental impacts (2023, open access) — A credible, citeable baseline for environmental impact discussions with cities and ESG teams.
  • Black Soldier Fly Larvae as a circular solution for organic waste management (2025, ScienceDirect) — Frames BSFL as infrastructure: reduces waste burden while producing saleable outputs (protein, oil, frass) in a circular model.
  • Black soldier fly larvae for organic manure recycling and its environmental benefits (ScienceDirect) — Especially relevant to farms and manure-heavy regions: positions BSFL as both a waste solution and a pathway to improved nutrient cycling.

Lifecycle assessment (LCA), climate & environmental performance

  • Black soldier fly for feed and food: a life cycle assessment (2019) — A widely referenced synthesis that helps teams benchmark BSFL vs. conventional ingredients and waste pathways in consistent LCA language.
  • Black soldier fly larvae as an alternative feed source and agro-waste bioconversion tool: a life cycle assessment based on feeding experiments (2023, Journal of Cleaner Production / ScienceDirect) — Links measured feeding experiments to LCA outcomes—excellent for “show me the data” stakeholders.
  • Sustainable use of Hermetia illucens insect biomass for feed and food (ScienceDirect) — Use this when you need one paper that speaks to sustainability, scalability, and product utility in the same breath.

Systems-level “keystone species” + operational optimization

  • Black Soldier Fly: A Keystone Species for the Future of Sustainable Bioeconomy (2025, open access) — The big-picture reference: ties together feed, waste, environment, and bioactive benefits into one cohesive bioeconomy argument.
  • Optimizing Black Soldier Fly (Hermetia illucens) production (2025, Frontiers) — Helps operators and partners align on the levers that drive outcomes: substrate strategy, rearing conditions, and throughput vs. quality tradeoffs.
  • Transforming waste, sustaining the future: a new guide to black soldier fly systems (2025, CCAC guide) — A planner-friendly reference for municipalities and project developers: system design, implementation considerations, and climate-aligned framing.

Processing, quality & product consistency

  • Quality characteristics of black soldier flies produced by different rearing substrates and processing methods (2023, open access) — Directly answers “how consistent is the product?” by comparing how substrate + processing shape quality traits buyers will test.
  • The processing-focused sections in Nutritional value of black soldier fly larvae processed by three different methods of treatment (2022, open access) — Useful when you’re discussing spray-dried vs. oven-dried tradeoffs (stability, handling, and application fit).
  • Improved nutritional and antioxidant properties of black soldier fly larvae through processing (2024, ScienceDirect) — Strengthens the “premium ingredient” narrative: processing can enhance properties that affect formulation performance.

Responsible production, welfare & risk management

  • Welfare considerations for farmed black soldier flies (Hermetia illucens) (Rethink Priorities) — A practical risk-and-governance reference: helps partners address welfare questions proactively and build credibility with auditors, buyers, and regulators as the sector scales.

Economics, deployment & social impact

  • Economic and environmental benefits from using black soldier fly larvae for waste management and smallholder production in Abidjan (2024, FAO) — A credible “implementation story” that speaks to practical deployment, job creation, and measurable waste-management co-benefits.
  • Black soldier fly for feed and food: a life cycle assessment – economic co-benefits section (cost, resource efficiency) in the Wageningen LCA report (2019) — Helpful when you need to connect environmental benefits to financial logic (resource efficiency, co-product value).
  • Economic and environmental benefits from using black soldier fly larvae (Hermetia illucens) in urban organic waste management systems (FAO/UN system note) — Frequently referenced by industry and city teams because it connects waste diversion to economic outcomes in clear, decision-ready language.
  • Transforming waste, sustaining the future: a new guide to black soldier fly systems – used by industry, development agencies, and city planners as a quasi-standard reference on BSF system design and benefits (2025) — The go-to when partners ask “what does a real system look like, and what benefits should we measure?”
