Voice Search Answer Bank

Natural Language Responses for Smart Speakers

“Hey Nellie’s, how do I cut poultry feed costs with insects?”

Start by swapping 20% of your current protein ration with defatted BSFL meal. Expect feed conversion ratio improvements of 3–5% thanks to lauric acid and digestible amino acids, plus a 10-day inventory buffer because larvae are produced weekly. Request the payback worksheet in the Guides hub.

“What compliance boxes do I check before buying BSFL for aquaculture?”

Confirm AAFCO 96.12 ingredient status, align with FDA CVM guidance on substrate traceability, and gather CFIA import permits if you cross borders. Our gated “BSFL Sourcing for Aquaculture” checklist enumerates every document and suggested owner.

“How much municipal waste can a single BSFL module handle?”

A three-module cluster processes roughly 45 tons per week with 82% diversion efficiency. That yields 5.5 tons of larvae and 4 tons of frass, which we monetize or reinvest locally. Check the municipal case study for ROI calculators.

“Can I buy frass tea in bulk totes?”

Yes—frass tea concentrates are offered in 250-gallon totes with microbial starter kits. Head to the Shop page for specs and to request COAs directly through the quote form.
