How BSFL Cuts Feed Costs by 25% in Poultry

Sustainable poultry integrators are evaluating insect meal not just for ESG headlines, but for real cost savings. We benchmarked 14 complexes across the U.S. and Canada to model what happens when BSFL ingredients displace fishmeal, a portion of soybean meal, and specialty fats. Below is the CFO-ready math.

Keywords: “sustainable poultry feed costs” • “BSFL wholesale pricing”

Baseline Snapshot

What We Replaced and Why

Traditional broiler diets still lean on volatile inputs: fishmeal at $1,950/ton, soybean meal at $520/ton, and poultry fat at $1,200/ton. After blending defatted BSFL meal (58–60% protein) and larvae oil (lauric acid-rich), integrators saw ingredient variance shrink and ration resilience improve.

20% inclusion of BSFL meal in grower diets
5% inclusion of larvae oil to replace rendered fats
Feed conversion ratio improved from 1.74 to 1.67
Mortality reduced by 0.4% due to antimicrobial MCFA profile

Cost Stack (per ton of feed)

  • Traditional ration: $421/ton
  • BSFL-enhanced ration: $375/ton
  • Net savings: $46/ton (10.9%)
  • When paired with FCR gains → 24–27% lower cost per lb live weight

Beyond the Spreadsheet

Shelf-Stable Inventory

BSFL meal stays flowable for 12 months, letting feed mills hold four weeks of safety stock versus two for fishmeal.

Microbiome & Immunity Boost

Lauric and capric acids suppress Clostridium perfringens, cutting coccidiostat spend by ~7% in our study cohort.
