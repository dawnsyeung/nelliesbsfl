Happy Earth Snapshot

What Happy Earth Delivers Today

Happy Earth provides curbside composting, recycling, and low-carbon services that make it easy for households and businesses to reduce waste and emissions. Residents receive branded buckets, schedule weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly swaps, and can request finished compost through the customer portal.

Current Service Highlights

  • Stylish indoor bin + outdoor bucket swap on pickup day.
  • Residential pricing range of $17-$39 based on pickup cadence.
  • Recycling services and commercial waste programs available.
  • Carbon offsets priced at $12 per ton with subscription options.
  • Renewable power plan evaluations delivered in 3-5 business days.

Partnership Model

How the On-Site System Works

Each deployment uses modular BSFL units sized to the local waste stream. Units include feedstock intake, larvae rearing racks, harvest stations, and frass finishing equipment with remote monitoring dashboards. Typical capacity ranges from 0.5 to 10 tons per day per module cluster.
